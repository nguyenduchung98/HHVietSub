# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

HHVietSub contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation in
the project respectful, constructive, and harassment-free.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Giving actionable technical feedback.
- Respecting different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Focusing discussion on the code, documentation, and user impact.
- Protecting users' private subtitles, media, credentials, and file paths.
- Accepting maintainer decisions made to preserve project safety and scope.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination,
publishing another person's private information, deliberately disruptive
activity, or sharing confidential user data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull
requests, and other contributions that violate these standards. Repeated or
serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project
spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as
confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when a person officially
represents HHVietSub in public community spaces.
